How it works

  • Every 15 seconds the station reads Squeekchain's public API (/v1/status): wheel state, RPM, rim speed, distance and the settled block height.
  • A WebAudio engine composes in real time: tempo, note density and brightness follow the live RPM. Running wheels get a driving pentatonic groove; a sleeping hamster gets ambient pads and sparse bells.
  • The wheel on this page spins at the hamster's real pace, and track titles are minted from the live block height — every block, a new track.
  • No wallet, no keys, no tokens, no transactions. Read-only telemetry in, music out.

About
